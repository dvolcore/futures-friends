# Futures Store: blind spots to close before the first real order

Short labels for these are on the store pages (the "Before you order" list). Everything marked **Draft** needs the owner and counsel to confirm. Nothing here is legal advice.

| # | Blind spot | What the site does now | What is still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **CPSIA Children's Product Certificates and tracking labels** for children's items (rugs and mats, plush, apparel, backpacks, posters are not children's products but any toy-like item is) | Plush and any soft toy are "Ships after safety testing" and cannot be ordered. Rugs say a flammability report and a certificate come first. | A CPSC-accepted lab test and a certificate per product, a permanent tracking label on each item, and a record of the batch. |
| 2 | **Small-parts warnings for under 3** | The label "Small parts, under 3" is on the store; printed puppets and plush carry the warning. | Confirm each item's warning with the lab report; no choking-size pieces in sets sold for under-3 rooms. |
| 3 | **Sales tax nexus (Missouri and Kansas)** | The site says tax is added on the invoice and never shows a tax figure. | Register for sales tax in Missouri and Kansas, turn on Stripe Tax, and check other states once sales volume crosses their thresholds. Ask the accountant about children's clothing and books. |
| 4 | **Shipping and freight for rugs** | Shipping is shown as a method and an estimate ("Quoted on your invoice"), never a number. Startup package shipping is part of the package price. | A freight carrier or broker, a quote process by ZIP, liftgate and no-dock handling, and damage claims. |
| 5 | **Returns and refunds** | Every page that sells says the policy is a draft. Made-to-order items are stated as not returnable unless damaged or wrong. | **Draft, owner and counsel to confirm.** Write the final policy. |
| 6 | **Terms of sale** | Linked as a draft from checkout. | **Draft, owner and counsel to confirm.** |
| 7 | **Tax-exempt orders from centers and churches** | Checkout collects a certificate number and state; the upload field is a placeholder. | A process to verify each certificate, keep it on file, and mark the Stripe customer exempt only after that. |
| 8 | **Privacy of order data** | The cart (ids and quantities) is saved on the visitor's device only. Name, address and PO are held in memory and sent only when a request is submitted. Children's data is not collected. | A retention period for order requests, and who may read them. Update the privacy policy when payments open. |
| 9 | **Fulfillment owner and inventory** | Every product is a draft with no stock count. | Name who packs and ships, where stock lives, and how a sold-out item is shown. |
| 10 | **Concept pictures** | Merchandise images are labelled "Concept sample, final product may vary"; room images carry the owner's concept caption. | Replace each with a real photograph once a sample exists, and keep the label until then. |
| 11 | **Lumi's eye color (open source-approval item)** | Not shown in copy. The campaign README records that the supplied sheet shows magenta eyes while older guidance differs. | The owner approves which source sheet is canon before any plush or apparel sample is sewn. |
| 12 | **Large square corner carpets** | Shown with "proposed sizes" (6 x 6 ft, 8 x 8 ft) and a note that they are not confirmed. | Maker confirms sizes, pile, backing, flammability class and price. |
| 13 | **Apparel** | Sizes and prices are "coming soon"; the form collects interest only. Hoodies are described as having no drawstrings. | Flammability (16 CFR 1610), tracking labels, drawstring rules for sizes 2T to 12, sizing chart, and a printed sample per product. |
| 14 | **Poster size and proofs** | $16 is shown for an 18 x 24 in poster, with "final print proofs come before printing". | The campaign README says final dimensions, bleed and color profile are not set; a printer's proof must match. |
