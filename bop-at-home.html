Bop at Home

Move Your Body, Grow Your Mind

Free family movement, no equipment and no scorekeeping. Eight short activities to do together, each with an adapted version, plus a weekly challenge you can join.

How Bop at Home works

  • Join inYou are part of the fun. Move with your child, and follow their lead.
  • Adapt freelyEvery activity has an adapted version, shown right on the card. Change anything so it works for your body, your space and your day.
  • Trying is the winWe celebrate taking part, not how well or how long. There are no scores and no comparing.

Free and short

Eight things to try tonight

Each one takes 30 seconds to 10 minutes and needs nothing but you. Clear a little space, stay close and offer water.

1

Elephant Stomp & Sway

2 min 38 secSmall indoor spaceMoveRhythm

  1. Stomp your heavy elephant feet: one, two, three.
  2. Swing your trunk from side to side.
  3. Slow down until you are a sleepy elephant.

Say: “Ready? Bop & Go!”

Adapted version

Seated or wheelchair: stomp with your hands on your knees or pat the arms of the chair, and swing your trunk with both arms. Sound-sensitive: do it in silence, or hum softly.

2

Trunk Reach

39 secAny spaceReach

  1. Spot an apple way up high.
  2. Stretch up on your tiptoes and reach your trunk up to pick it.
  3. Reach once more for another apple. Yum!

Say: “How high can your trunk go today?”

Adapted version

Reach from a chair, with one arm, or by pointing with your eyes or your voice.

3

Freeze & Try Again

1 min 9 secSmall indoor spaceControl

  1. Move any way you like while you sing or hum.
  2. When the song stops, freeze like a statue.
  3. Wiggle out of it and try again.

Say: “Freeze! Now try again.”

Adapted version

Freeze just your hands, or just your face. If a child cannot hear the song stop, wave a hand or flick the lights as the signal.

4

Animal Walks

1 min 9 secLarge indoor spaceMoveBalance

  1. Pick an animal: Booker bear, Lumi bunny, Zuri turtle or Bop elephant.
  2. Move like that animal across the room.
  3. Call "Switch!" and pick a new animal.

Say: “Which animal will you be?”

Adapted version

Choose the animal that fits your body today. Use your arms and face for the animal, or roll, scoot or wheel across the room.

5

Flamingo Balance

1 min 9 secSmall indoor spaceBalance

  1. Stand tall like a flamingo and hold a grown-up's hand or the wall.
  2. Lift one foot a little and count to five together.
  3. Switch feet. Wobbles are part of it.

Say: “Wobbles are welcome.”

Adapted version

Sit tall and lift one foot, or lift both arms. Hold a grown-up's hands, or stand on two feet and just "grow tall."

6

Clap-Back Rhythm

1 min 9 secAny spaceRhythm

  1. A grown-up claps a short pattern.
  2. Clap it back.
  3. Swap: you make a pattern and the grown-up claps it back.

Say: “Your turn to lead!”

Adapted version

Pat your knees, tap the floor, stomp or make sounds. If a child cannot hear the claps, use big arm movements or a gentle tap on the shoulder.

7

Outside Quest: Find Three

10 minOutdoors, with a grown-upMoveReach

  1. Step outside and pick a color.
  2. Find three things that color and tell what you notice.
  3. Walk, skip or roll between finds, and take a water break.

Say: “What do you notice?”

Adapted version

Window Quest: find your three things from a window or inside the house. Take rest stops whenever you like, and use a wagon, stroller or chair on smooth paths.

8

Lumi's Slow Reset

3 minAny spaceReset

  1. Notice: is your body speedy or quiet?
  2. Smell the flower, blow out the candle: three slow breaths.
  3. Soften your voice and your hands, and rest as long as you like.

Say: “Speedy body or quiet body?”

Adapted version

Offered, never forced. Breathe with your eyes open, rest a hand on your tummy or hold a soft toy, or simply listen to the quiet.

A grown-up joins in and supervises every activity. Stop whenever a child is tired, and never use movement or outdoor time as a reward or a punishment.
