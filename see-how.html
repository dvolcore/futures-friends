See how: picture guides

For parents who would rather see it than read about it. Eight everyday routines, one picture per step. Press play to walk through the steps, or print a guide for the fridge.

Picture guides

Routines in pictures

Each guide uses the same words your child hears in a Futures classroom, so home and school sound alike.

Smell the flower, blow the candle

When feelings get big, before bed, or any time

  1. 1Hold up a pretend flower.
  2. 2Smell it slowly: breathe in through your nose.
  3. 3Hold up a pretend candle.
  4. 4Blow it out slowly through your mouth.
  5. 5Do it three times, together.

Booker's brave reader steps

When your child meets a new word

  1. 1Look at the picture: what do you know about it?
  2. 2Look at every letter, not just the first: k-i-t-e.
  3. 3Slide a finger under the word and say each sound: /k/ /ī/ /t/. The quiet e helps i say its name.
  4. 4Blend: kite! Not quite? "Big breath. Brave heart. I can try again!"

Zuri's wonder loop

For any question your child asks

  1. 1Notice: "What do you see?"
  2. 2Predict: "What do you think will happen?"
  3. 3Try: do it together.
  4. 4Compare: "Was it what you thought?"

Ready? Bop & Go! movement break

After sitting, between activities, or when energy is high

  1. 1Stomp your elephant feet.
  2. 2Swing your trunk side to side.
  3. 3Freeze like a statue.
  4. 4Take a water break.
  5. 5Slow down: one big breath.

Bedtime wind-down: notice, breathe, soften, rest

The last 10 minutes before sleep

  1. 1Notice: "What can you hear?"
  2. 2Breathe: two slow flower breaths.
  3. 3Soften: soft hands, soft shoulders, soft face.
  4. 4Rest: lights low, same song, good night.

Front, back, top, sparkle!

Morning and bedtime brushing

  1. 1Toothpaste: rice-grain smear under 3, pea-sized from 3.
  2. 2Front: small circles.
  3. 3Back: small circles.
  4. 4Top: the chewing part.
  5. 5Spit, then a big sparkle smile.

Count colors, not bites

At meals and snacks

  1. 1Serve the meal as usual.
  2. 2Name the colors you see together.
  3. 3Count the colors, never the bites.
  4. 4Your child decides what and how much to eat.

Tooth-brushing amounts follow the AAP (opens in a new tab); bedtime routines follow the AAP sleep guidance (opens in a new tab); mealtime choice follows our Eat the Rainbow rule: food is never a reward or a punishment.
