Family App guide

What you will see in the Family App

A quick tour: signing in, what shows up each day, and where to find your reports.

Signing in

Four steps

Get your invite

Your center sends an email invitation to the address on your enrollment form.

Verify

Enter the code sent to your phone or email.

Add your details

Confirm your name and how you want to be notified.

Turn on quick sign-in

Use Face ID, fingerprint or a PIN next time.

Today at Futures

The lesson, the lead friend and a question to ask at dinner.

What we ate

Breakfast, lunch and snack, plus new foods your child tried.

Friendship Stars

Stars your child earned and why.

Take-home activity

A five-minute activity for the week.

Learning Steps

What teachers saw, in words, with what comes next and an idea to try at home.

Messages

Notes from your child's teacher, and replies.
