Booker Tries Again

Finished coverIllustrated · 18 pagesLEARN · Confidence

Booker finds a new book with a word he does not know yet. Lumi helps him look at all four letters of k-i-t-e, where the quiet e helps i say its name, and they read it together. Then Booker welcomes his little sister into story time before she can read.

Lumi’s Big Feelings, Brighter Days

Finished coverIllustrated · 18 pagesBELONG · Kindness

Lumi notices Pip, the new little hedgehog, standing quietly by the fence during the Parade game. Instead of deciding how Pip feels, she asks. A slow breath helps her feel ready even with a wobbly tummy, and a kind invitation, at Pip's own pace, brightens Pip's day.

Zuri: What Happens If We Try?

Finished coverIllustrated · 18 pagesEXPLORE · Curiosity

An ice cube disappears from the sunny porch, leaving only a puddle. Zuri and her friends make guesses, set up three ice cubes about the same size, and watch closely: ice melts when it gets warm enough, and water freezes when it gets cold enough. No winner, just what they saw, and a brand-new question.

Bop’s Clean-Up Team

Finished coverIllustrated · 18 pagesMOVE · Independence

After a big morning of play, Bop sees the biggest mess in the Clubhouse. He starts with one small thing, tries again when the books slide down, lends Zuri a hand (and a trunk), and the whole clean-up team finishes the job together.

FF101 · Week 1

The Wobbly Welcome Tower

BookerLEARNSMILE

Booker sees the tower wobble and stays quiet. After it tumbles, he says what he noticed and tries his own fix.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeHave you ever built a tower that wobbled? What did you do?
  2. DuringPoint to the narrow block! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhich block went on the bottom? Why?
Feeling wordunsureNot knowing what will happen. Booker felt unsure, and tried anyway.
Try at homeStack things you already have, like boxes, plastic cups or books. Try a wide bottom, then a narrow bottom. Which one is steadier?

Words to use: wide · narrow · steady

Say it together: “I don’t know yet — but I can try.”

FF102 · Week 2

A Spot for Everyone

LumiBELONGRESET

Lumi builds a story fort and picks every spot herself. It only works when she asks each friend and waits for the answer.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhere do you like to sit when we read a story?
  2. DuringWhere would YOU sit? Point! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhere is the open side of the fort? Why did Lumi leave it open?
Feeling wordwelcomeThe warm feeling when someone makes room for you.
Try at homeBuild a blanket fort with one side left open. Ask each person, "Is your spot right for you?" and wait for the answer. Keep blankets off faces.

Words to use: spot · inside · open

Say it together: “Notice. Ask. Listen.”

Episode in development: not made yet. From the Futures Friends Series Bible v3.0 (s.6.2 and Appendix F).
FF103 · Week 3

What Melts?

ZuriEXPLORENOURISH

Zuri tries every ice rescue at once and cannot tell what worked, until she runs one fair test: sun or shade.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat do you think happens to ice in a sunny spot?
  2. DuringSun or shade: which melts first? Point! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhich melted first? How did Zuri know?
Feeling wordcuriousWanting to find out. Zuri says, "I wonder… let’s find out!"
Try at homeFreeze a big toy (too big to swallow) in two cups of water. Put one cup in the sun and one in the shade. Guess first, then check. Ice stays out of mouths.

Words to use: melt · warm · predict

Say it together: “I wonder… let’s find out!”

Episode in development: not made yet. From the Futures Friends Series Bible v3.0 (s.6.2 and Appendix F).
FF104 · Week 4

The Cleanup Beat

BopMOVEOUTSIDE

Bop cleans the whole room alone, fast, and builds Pile-Mountain. His clap-clap-PUT beat turns cleanup into a team job.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeHow do we clean up our toys at home?
  2. DuringClap-clap-PUT with Bop! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat worked better than piling everything up?
Feeling wordproudThe good feeling after a job done together.
Try at homeClean up to a beat: clap, clap, PUT one toy away. Sort toys into boxes by picture or color. Everyone gets a part.

Words to use: sort · pattern · match

Say it together: “I can do it — and we can do it together.” Move Your Body, Grow Your Mind

FF105 · Week 5

The Missing Rhyme

BookerLEARNSMILE

The last page is missing. Booker un-says every ending he tries, until he picks one and keeps it.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat words sound like "cat"?
  2. DuringWhat rhymes with BEAR? Silly words count! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat rhymes with your favorite animal?
Feeling wordnervousA wiggly feeling before you try something. Booker laughed, picked a word and kept it.
Try at homePlay rhyme ping-pong: say a word, then take turns saying words that rhyme. Silly words count. Rhyme with things, never with people’s names.

Words to use: rhyme · sound · end

Say it together: “I don’t know yet — but I can try.”

FF106 · Week 6

The Feelings Forecast

LumiBELONGRESET

Rain cancels Booker’s Story Walk. Lumi’s Fix-It Menu cannot fix his disappointment, so she stops fixing and stays with him.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeHow does your face look when you feel disappointed?
  2. DuringShow me a disappointed face… now calm. (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat did Lumi do when the menu did not work?
Feeling worddisappointedSad because something you wanted did not happen. The feeling can stay a while, and that is okay.
Try at homeDo a morning "feelings forecast": everyone names one feeling. If someone feels low, ask, "Do you want to be quiet together?" and wait. Sharing is always your child’s choice.

Words to use: feeling · disappointed · calm

Say it together: “Notice. Ask. Listen.”

FF107 · Week 7

Seed Surprise

ZuriEXPLORENOURISH

Zuri’s seed will not sprout, so her "help" keeps growing. When she stops to watch and draw, she sees the root grow first.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat do you think a seed needs to grow?
  2. DuringRoot: up or down? Point! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat grew first, the root or the sprout?
Feeling wordpatientWaiting calmly. Zuri learned to look, wait and draw what she saw.
Try at homeA grown-up tucks a dry bean against the side of a clear cup of damp paper towel. Look each day and draw what you see. Beans and soil stay away from mouths; for children under 4, keep the cup sealed in a clear bag.

Words to use: seed · root · sprout

Say it together: “I wonder… let’s find out!”

Episode in development: not made yet. From the Futures Friends Series Bible v3.0 (s.6.2 and Appendix F).
FF108 · Week 8

Move Like Me

BopMOVEOUTSIDE

Bop’s warm-up is too fast to follow. When Rory leads a slow spin, Bop slows his whole body down so everyone can join.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeHow do you warm up your body before you play?
  2. DuringStomp, stomp, FREEZE! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat did Bop do instead of going fast?
Feeling wordexcitedA big, bouncy feeling. Bop learned to slow it down so friends could follow.
Try at homeTake turns leading a slow warm-up: "First we stomp, then we…" Everyone copies, then your child leads. Move your own way; no spinning until dizzy.

Words to use: first · then · under

Say it together: “I can do it — and we can do it together.” Move Your Body, Grow Your Mind

FF109 · Week 9

The Rainbow Snack Adventure

All four friendsNOURISHOUTSIDE

The friends split up to fill a rainbow snack board and bring back three bananas. Only together, by asking, do they find purple.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat colors can you find on your plate today?
  2. DuringCan you see something purple? Point! (pause once)
  3. AfterWho knew where purple grows?
Feeling wordbraveTrying something new. Looking and smelling count, and tasting is always a choice.
Try at homeMake a rainbow board: find one food for each color at home or at the store. Look, smell and touch. Tasting is your child’s choice. Shred or cook hard vegetables soft for under-5s.

Words to use: purple · smell · crunchy

FF110 · Week 10

The Brave Question

BookerLEARNSMILE

Booker’s book is stuck in his zipped backpack. He refuses help, misses his turn, then asks, "Can you help me, please?"

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat can you do when something is stuck?
  2. DuringWill pulling HARDER work? Thumbs up or down! (pause once)
  3. AfterWho could YOU ask for help?
Feeling wordfrustratedThe hot, stuck feeling when something will not work. Asking for help is brave.
Try at homePractice the brave question with a tricky zipper or lid: "Can you help me, please?" At bedtime, ask each other, "Who helped you today?"

Words to use: help · stuck · ask

Say it together: “I don’t know yet — but I can try.”

FF111 · Week 11

Two Friends, One Toy

LumiBELONGRESET

Lumi and Zuri both need the toy crane. Lumi keeps giving in, then grabs. Kindness has to include her own turn too.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat can you do when two people want the same toy?
  2. DuringMy turn, your turn: point! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat helped them take turns?
Feeling wordmadA hot, tight feeling. Lumi took three calm breaths, then said, "I want a turn too."
Try at homeShare one toy with a timer or by counting to 20. Practice saying, "I want a turn too" and "Your turn, my turn." When someone feels mad, try three slow breaths together first.

Words to use: turn · wait · next

Say it together: “Notice. Ask. Listen.”

Episode in development: not made yet. From the Futures Friends Series Bible v3.0 (s.6.2 and Appendix F).
FF112 · Week 12

Where Did My Shadow Go?

ZuriEXPLORENOURISH

Zuri’s shadow vanishes and she pretends to know why. Saying "I don’t know yet" helps her find the cloud, and a new mystery.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhere do you think shadows come from?
  2. DuringMake a shadow with your hand! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhat is still a mystery?
Feeling wordpuzzledNot knowing the answer yet. It is okay to keep wondering.
Try at homeOn a sunny day, trace each other’s shadows with chalk and watch what happens when a cloud passes. Indoors, make hand shadows with a flashlight. Never look at the sun; keep the light out of eyes.

Words to use: shadow · light · block

Say it together: “I wonder… let’s find out!”

Episode in development: not made yet. From the Futures Friends Series Bible v3.0 (s.6.2 and Appendix F).
FF113 · Week 13

The Brighter Together Parade

All four friendsLEARNBELONGEXPLOREMOVE

The friends lead a parade for their families, and every job goes wobbly. Each friend fixes one using another friend’s strength.

Talk about it
  1. BeforeWhat is each friend good at?
  2. DuringOpen book, heart hands, compass point, sun stretch! (pause once)
  3. AfterWhose strength did you use today?
Feeling wordjoyfulA big, happy feeling you share with others.
Try at homeHold a family parade around your home. Each person does one friend’s move: Open Book, Heart Hands, Compass Point and Sun Stretch. Walk, do not run.

Words to use: together · strength · parade
