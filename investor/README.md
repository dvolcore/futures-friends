# Futures Friends investor evidence room

**Current package date:** 11 October 2026
**Use:** public-facing index for the investor review package. The linked files are working materials and should be read with their status notes.

Futures Friends combines early-learning content, family support and an operating platform for daycare and church partners. The package describes the current product direction, production system, operating model and funding controls. It does not promise accreditation, completed deployment, a fixed production quote or a guaranteed investor return.

The production gate is an accepted seven-minute episode, two QR assets and one bilingual promo. This gate protects the investor model from treating generated clips as finished episodes.

## Readiness and open gates

The package is ready for investor discussion as a documented plan with explicit assumptions. The following items remain gates before the related claim can be described as delivered:

- written vendor quotes and rights terms for animation, voices, music, localization, captions and finishing;
- pilot acceptance by education, native-Spanish, accessibility, safeguarding and rights reviewers;
- production source ownership, storage, derivatives, platform ingest and revision terms;
- legal review of child privacy, data handling, contracts, insurance and merchandise safety;
- live hosting, role isolation, persistence, payment and support verification for any operational-platform claim;
- confirmation of enrollment, renewal and partner conversion evidence before forecast scenarios are treated as performance.

The complete original business plan, Bible and operations companion are included in the branded PDFs as historical references. Current controls appear first and supersede their earlier financial and readiness assumptions.

## Evidence convention

Each document distinguishes current published direction, draft work, archival material, allowance, published price, observed account evidence and quote-required scope. Reviewers should follow those labels when comparing the story Bible, operating companion, platform claims and financial model. The package is designed to make the next verification step visible rather than turning an assumption into an accomplishment.
