Owner-set budgets: food capped at $2,000.00/month (Missouri meal pattern still met); toiletries and cleaning within $300.00/month ($3,110.18/year with paper towels halved); classroom and office consumables halved to $1,599.87/year; utilities locked at $1,500.00/month; tax preparation $1,000.00/year; curriculum mapping $7,500.00 and native Spanish review $1,500.00, one-time, with no recurring curriculum or Spanish review fee; RN consultation $2,000.00/year; administration cut to $5,872.00/year (part-time bookkeeper); building upkeep handled in-house (lawn, HVAC service, electrical, parking and staff mobile removed; snow halved). The owner-stated $140,000 refinance-enabling need is separate. The estimated pre-financing cash need is $347,911.98, assuming the stated enrollment ramp, receipts and cash floor. This is not an approved or demonstrated financeable loan ask.

The locked-in $210/week ($910/month) tuition at 38 children and 96% collections generates $398,361.60 annually and an operating margin of $112,354.23 before building payments or debt, with the owner as unpaid director. Break-even tuition is $651.16 before building payments and debt. Many costs are one-time: $142,554.00 one-time (months 1–7) vs $286,007.37/year recurring. Net cash turns positive in month 6; cumulative pilot cash turns positive in month 24 at the month-12 rate, before building payments and debt. Borrowed operating cash is not repayment revenue. The building payment, actual lender terms and prior creator invoice remain unknown.

All-day care for 38 children ages 2–5: four full-time caregivers and a part-time floater, all at $16/hour with no employer health benefit; the owner serves as director, unpaid; no food worker and no paid substitutes. Staffing is conditional on age mix, room grouping, licensing, hours and actual attendance. Published prices and planning allowances are not written supplier quotations.
