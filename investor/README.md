## Financial basis

Model version pilot-38-v5 (staffing and tuition owner-confirmed October 13, 2026): annual recurring costs $330,018.06; one-time setup $145,554.00; setup contingency $21,833.10; gross first-year spend $497,405.16. The owner-stated $140,000 refinance-enabling need is separate. The estimated pre-financing cash need is $317,451.41, assuming the stated enrollment ramp, receipts and cash floor. This is not an approved or demonstrated financeable loan ask.

The locked-in $210/week ($910/month) tuition at 38 children and 96% collections generates $398,361.60 annually and an operating margin of $68,343.54 before building payments or debt, with the owner as unpaid director. Break-even tuition is $752.55 before building payments and debt. Borrowed operating cash is not repayment revenue. The building payment, actual lender terms and prior creator invoice remain unknown.

All-day care for 38 children ages 2–5: four full-time caregivers and a part-time floater, all at $16/hour with no employer health benefit; the owner serves as director, unpaid; no food worker and no paid substitutes. Staffing is conditional on age mix, room grouping, licensing, hours and actual attendance. Published prices and planning allowances are not written supplier quotations.
