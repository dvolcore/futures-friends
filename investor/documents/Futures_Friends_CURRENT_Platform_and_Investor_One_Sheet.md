# Futures Friends | Platform + Investor One-Sheet
## A fuller plan. A costed path forward. | 11 October 2026

### The opportunity
Families and early-learning operators need one trusted experience that joins classroom practice, home support, movement, nutrition, stories, and measurable progress. Futures Friends brings those pieces together through a bilingual character world built for ages 2–5.

### The product
**Booker learns. Lumi belongs. Zuri explores. Bop moves.** The program combines curriculum, stories, printables, family activities, Eat the Rainbow planning, movement, learning-zone kits, branded merchandise, and a planned hosted platform.

A production platform is designed around director, teacher, parent, adult-mediated Kid Mode, support, and partner roles. It is intended to support curriculum work, progress records, parent communication, approved character messages, billing, and operations. The public site demonstrates the brand and content direction; hosted operation, security, privacy, backups, billing, and real-device acceptance remain gated work.

### First operating case
One pilot location, ages 2–5. Expansion to other daycares, churches, home daycares, families, licensing, and merchandise is future revenue and is not contracted in the current model.

### Capital request
**Proposed: $1,500,000 no-equity loan concept.** Terms, rate, draw schedule, collateral, covenants, repayment, points, and revenue participation require negotiation and counsel review. No permanent ownership is offered in this concept. No return is guaranteed.

The illustrative schedule models 12% interest-only for 12 months at $15,000/month, then approximately $33,366.67/month for 60 months. Receipts are unsecured stress inputs. Base year-one net receipts are $370,784.80 and operating cash before debt is -$874,057.38. Current stress cases do not demonstrate repayment coverage. Funding must be staged against evidence, acceptance, and verified demand.

### Why it can win
Owned characters and curriculum; bilingual family reach; physical learning zones connected to digital content; adult-mediated child safety; a platform that can serve centers, churches, and families; and multiple future revenue paths across subscriptions, implementation, training, curriculum, tablets, kits, merchandise, and licensing.

### What the investor should verify next
License and capacity, host agreement, entity/EIN/insurance, staff and payroll, enrollment/attendance/collections, bank and debt records, vendor quotes, rights chain, privacy/security review, hosted role flows, backups/restores, content approvals, and signed partner demand.

**Decision frame:** fund a gated pilot completion and validation plan, or revise scope and capital before committing to the full model. The detailed business plan, Production Bible, Operations Companion, cost workbook, forecast, and historical source PDFs provide the diligence trail.
