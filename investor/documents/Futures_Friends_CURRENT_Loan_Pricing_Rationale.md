# Loan pricing rationale — 11 October 2026

## Proposed terms

$1,500,000 facility; 12% fixed nominal annual interest; 12 interest-only months followed by 60 amortizing months. This is a negotiation starting point, not a quoted offer, a published market average, or a promise that financing is available. No equity, conversion, perpetual royalty or permanent ownership is offered. No points or fees are included in this illustration.

## Evidence and judgment

The Federal Reserve H.15 release dated 9 October 2026 reports bank prime at 7%. Source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h15/ . The proposal is therefore prime plus five percentage points at this observation date; its rate would remain fixed if agreed.

SBA 7(a) variable loans above $350,000 have a maximum base rate plus three percentage points. A prime-based example is 10% at the observed prime rate. This is a government-guaranteed lending ceiling, not a quote or eligibility finding for this startup. Source: https://www.sba.gov/loans/7a-loans/ .

Hercules Capital reported a 12.0% core portfolio yield for Q2 2026, a 12.0%–12.5% forward expectation, and an 86.8% first-lien mix. Portfolio yield includes different economics from a loan coupon, and its borrowers differ from this project. This is context, not a direct comparable quote. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1280784/000128078426000043/htgcq22026release.htm .

Accion Opportunity Fund publishes a 9.99%–28.99% term-loan interest range. Its eligibility, loan sizes and underwriting differ; the range does not establish availability of $1.5M here. Source: https://aofund.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/38137382987405-What-are-the-interest-rates-for-AOF-s-term-loans .

A proposed 12% coupon recognizes higher risk than a guaranteed bank loan without adopting the upper end of small-business financing. Its suitability depends on actual collateral, cash generation, borrower financials, guarantees, draw conditions and recovery rights. An investor can reasonably decline even at 12% if those protections are insufficient.

## Funding and repayment gates

All four current forecast scenarios fail repayment coverage. The 12% proposal does not fix that deficiency. $1.5M is also below the $3,139,152.61 first-year gross planning scope, before receipts; 554 cost rows remain allowances requiring records or quotes. The itemized operating-cost ledger is unchanged by this rate revision.

Prefer separately approved draws tied to accepted milestones and documented costs. Charge interest only on drawn principal, with a fresh schedule for each agreed draw structure. Do not represent the full-draw illustration as the schedule for staged financing.

Before signing, agree collateral and lien priority, any guarantees, payment start, maturity, reporting, cash covenants, draw conditions, default remedies, prepayment rules, fees, rounding and final payment. Document the actual repayment source and the additional capital required. The current proposal leaves these terms unagreed; legal review is required for the executed documents.
