Financial discipline

No repayment
from borrowed operating cash.

The $140,000 refinance need is shown separately from pilot operations. Debt capacity is calculated only from cash remaining after wages, food, platform, insurance, taxes, ongoing operating costs and the verified building payment. The mortgage payment is currently open; no financeability claim is made until the lender terms are known.

Decision gate: enter the actual mortgage payment in the full model. It calculates the maximum debt payment at 1.25× coverage and shows when 40-child tuition cannot support the requested facility.
