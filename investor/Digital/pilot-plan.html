    Ratio gate: Missouri ratios depend on the actual age mix and room grouping. Two-year-olds require 1 adult per 8 children (maximum group 16); preschool groups require 1 per 10 (maximum group 20), subject to the applicable five-year-old rule and license. Four floor adults assumes two approved preschool groups of 20. Eight two-year-olds plus two older groups of 16 require five floor adults; an additional 10-hour post costs at least $50,000 in annual wages before taxes and relief. This schedule is a planning case, not a license approval. The director only counts in ratio while qualified and assigned to the room; food, clerical and relief hours do not count unless legally assigned.
