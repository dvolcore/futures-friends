Why the proposed rate is 12%

12% is a negotiation starting point, not a published market average or lender quote. Current Federal Reserve prime is 7%; the SBA guaranteed-loan ceiling and specialist lending yields reflect different risks. A full-draw loan needs $33,098.88 monthly cash before debt for 1.25× coverage after month 24. All current scenarios fail that test.
