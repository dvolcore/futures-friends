The active investor case is a 40-child, one-location pilot. Staffing, tuition, recurring costs and debt capacity are shown in the current model with open assumptions clearly marked.

Pilot scope

Refinance need

Owner-stated need shown separately. Building payment and lender terms remain unknown.

Affordability gate

The $910 tuition baseline leaves an annual operating shortfall of $57,738.02. Operating break-even is $1,036.37 per child per month at 40 children and 96% collections, before building payments and debt.

Debt capacity is tested only after wages, food, platform, insurance, taxes, recurring costs and the verified building payment. No financeability or repayment claim is made while that payment is unknown.

The assumptions behind the pilot.

Center cash · modeled

One pilot location serves ages 2–5. Tuition, capacity, staffing, collections, owner pay and operating costs remain planning assumptions until verified against licenses, contracts and bank records.

Partner cash · Phase 2

Paid daycare and church expansion is deferred. No signed partner revenue is assumed in the current pilot cash forecast or repayment test. Price and scope must be agreed before including it in a future financing model.

Debt service · pending verification

The refinance need is shown separately from the pilot operating case. Building payment, lender rate, term, closing costs and draw schedule are unknown. The model will test debt only after those inputs are verified and after ongoing operating costs.

Coverage is a planning test

Cash before debt ÷ monthly lender payment. 1.25× is a planning threshold, not lender approval. Changing the controls tests sensitivity; it does not establish demand or operating capability.
