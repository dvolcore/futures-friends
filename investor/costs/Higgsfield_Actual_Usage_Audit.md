## Why a $3,000 finished-season line is insufficient

Observed Seedance 2.5 1080p/high/audio examples cost 48 credits for four seconds and 360 credits for thirty seconds: **12 credits per generated second**. One pass of the 84-minute episode season consumes 60,480 credits, or $2,842.56 proportionally; whole 5,000-credit packs require 13 packs, or $3,055. The 102-minute package (episodes, QR/home clips and promos) consumes 73,440 credits, or $3,451.68 proportionally; whole packs require 15, or $3,525.

Those figures omit rejected shots, revisions, editorial, music, voices, Spanish delivery, educator review, captions and legal clearance. They therefore explain why a $3,000 finished-season claim is not supportable.
