# Futures Friends operating-record evidence audit

Audit date: 2026-10-11  
Scope: local Downloads project records, investor/business-plan records, and relevant Desktop files. This is a read-only evidence review. Forecasts, proposed rates, targets, and owner-confirmation prompts are not treated as actual operating records.

> **Current model (13 October 2026):** this audit reviews the records that existed on 11 October. The capacities, staffing scenarios and payroll figures it quotes from older proposals (40, 41 and 50 children; Option A/B staffing) are historical evidence, not the plan. The current model is pilot-38-v5.1: 38 children ages 2–5 in all-day care, the owner as unpaid director, 4 full-time caregivers and a part-time floater at $16/hour, owner-confirmed locked-in tuition of $210/week ($910/month), and a year-two operating margin of $112,354.23 before building payments and debt. The older proposals cited below were moved to `99_OUTDATED_TO_REMOVE/superseded_investor_models_2026-10-13/` in the project folder. The audit's finding stands: no primary operating record (license, roster, payroll, ledger, bank, debt) has been supplied.

## Executive finding

The reviewed package does not contain a current primary record proving licensed capacity, enrollment, attendance, tuition collections, staffing/payroll, rent, cash, reserves, or debt. The package itself says those records must be supplied before sending. The only enrollment figure found is an explicitly old snapshot of three children; it must not be reported as current. The operating cost model therefore remains a scenario/allowance model, not a statement of present operations.

`FUTURES_FRIENDS_PROJECT/99_OUTDATED_TO_REMOVE/superseded_investor_models_2026-10-13/01_Investor/v4.3_draft/INVESTOR_PROPOSAL_v4.3_DRAFT.md` states the proposed location as 3625 S Blue Ridge Blvd, Independence, MO 64052 (line 68), ages 2–5 (lines 32 and 72), and tuition of $210/week (about $910/month) (line 73). ### Historical or forecast figures

* Capacity is unresolved: v4.3 lines 10, 17 and 294 show 50 planned, 41 owner-set, and licensed capacity unknown; the original plan also discusses 40. The business-plan PDF p. 4 says 40, 41 and 50 conflict and none should be presented as current licensed capacity.
* Hours are unresolved. v4.3 line 68 leaves hours to be confirmed; the older flagship claims of Monday–Friday 7:00–6:00 are identified in v4.3 line 437 as not confirmed.
* Enrollment: the original business-plan PDF p. 4 describes an old snapshot of three children and says it is not current traction. v4.3 lines 44–54 labels “children enrolled today: 3” as `[OWNER: confirm]`. No current roster, attendance report, tuition ledger or matched deposits was found.
* Staffing/payroll: v4.3 lines 224–232 models approximately $23,500/month payroll for the 50-child scenario and approximately $9,200/month for the 41-child scenario. Lines 294–304 mark wage rates, taxes/WC and related costs for confirmation. These are modeled staffing cases, not a staff roster or payroll register.
* Rent/occupancy: v4.3 lines 68 and 304 use $1,500/month as a model input while requiring the host agreement/rent confirmation. No signed lease or use agreement was found.
* Food/utilities: v4.3 lines 294–304 uses $70/child/month or $2,000/month food and $300/$1,500 utilities as scenario values, with “actual” values still requiring owner confirmation. They are not invoices or bank-backed actuals.
* Collections: v4.3 line 311 models 94–97% collections; the original plan p. 5 says 100% billed tuition is the base and a 4% shortfall is only downside sensitivity. Neither is current delinquency or deposit evidence.
* Cash/reserves: the original plan pp. 7–8 and its cash bridge present modeled operating cash and reserve/financing cases. The plan says owner cash contribution is unknown pending bank statements and a contributed-cost ledger (p. 7). No bank statements, cash count or reserve account evidence was found.
* Debt: v4.3 line 40 proposes $140,000 at 9% and marks amount/rate for owner confirmation. The original investor business plan requests $240,000 of business debt (p. 2 / financing request section) and contains a separate larger financing alternative. These are financing proposals, not existing debt records; the amount conflict must be resolved before any debt schedule or repayment claim is used.

## Material conflicts to preserve in the model/package

1. **Licensed capacity:** 40, 41 and 50 appear across legacy/current materials. Use “licensed capacity unverified” until the license and occupancy/fire records are attached; do not select 40, 41 or 50 as actual.
2. **Enrollment:** “three children” is a dated/old snapshot and an owner-confirmation prompt, not current enrollment. Require a dated roster, attendance report and tuition ledger matched to deposits.
3. **Hours/opening date:** 7:00–6:00 and “opened September 2026” are old/unconfirmed claims. Require a current schedule and opening/licensing record.
4. **Financing:** $140,000 at 9% in v4.3 conflicts with $240,000 business debt in the original plan; neither proves existing borrowing. Require signed note/commitment and current debt schedule.
5. **Host occupancy:** the address is stated, but host church identity, written use/lease terms and rent remain unverified. Do not treat $1,500/month as actual rent.
6. **Staff compliance:** the lower-cost 41-child option is expressly warned as potentially below ratio and omits a director salary (v4.3 lines 57 and 372–373). It cannot be used as an actual payroll or compliant operating plan without roster, credentials, duty hours and ratio evidence.

## Records still required before calling operations factual

* DESE license or exemption/application, licensed capacity, age mix, inspection/fire/occupancy approval, and current operating hours.
* Signed host-church lease/use agreement naming the host, term, rent, utilities and permitted use.
* Legal entity, EIN, ownership, insurance and tax filings.
* Current staff roster; director credentials/duty schedule; wage rates/hours; payroll register; contractor list; payroll taxes and workers’ compensation records; background-check status.
* Dated enrollment roster, attendance, deposits, tuition ledger, receivables, discounts, subsidy/CACFP records and bank deposit tie-out.
* Bank statements, cash-on-hand proof, reserves, owner-contribution ledger, accounts payable, signed debt notes and current debt schedule.
* Food/kitchen or caterer permits, invoices and any CACFP participation record.

Until those records are produced, the cost model should label all capacity, staffing, rent, food, utilities, collections, cash, reserves and financing figures as forecast, allowance, benchmark or quote-required scope. No primary record in this reviewed set was found that can safely replace those assumptions.
