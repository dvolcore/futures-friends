Photo and video release, continued

I understand that:

  • Images will never include my child's last name, birthday, address or medical information.
  • Images will not be sold. They may be used only to share and promote Futures Learning Center and the Futures Friends program.
  • Photos shared in the family app are kept for one year and then deleted.
  • Once something is posted publicly, others may see or share it. If I change my mind, the center will remove posts it controls within 7 days, but cannot recall printed pieces already handed out.
  • I can change my level at any time by telling the director in writing.
  • Location tags are turned off on any post that shows a child, and the center never tags a parent's personal account without permission.
