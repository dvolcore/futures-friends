Futures Friends at Futures Learning Center

More than a place to stay. A place to learn.

In your child's day

  • Short Futures Friends stories with Booker, Lumi, Zuri and Bop, then hands-on learning
  • Four learning zones: reading, calm and feelings, discovery, and movement
  • Outdoor play, music, cooking and art
  • Meals and snacks with our Eat the Rainbow color names
  • A quiet rest time

For your family

  • A Futures Friends welcome kit to take home
  • Free Futures at Home read-alongs, activities and printables on our website
  • Help getting a public library card for your child (ask for our library sheet)
  • A tour and a real person to answer your questions
