The year

12 monthly units, 48 theme weeks

Each month has a theme and four weeks, each week led by one friend. Units 1, 2, 3 and 4 are written day by day (draft); Units 5 to 12 are outlined week by week. The full plans are for licensed centers. See the year at a glance

By age

One plan, three age versions

Every activity has a version for each age band, so mixed-age rooms and home daycares use the same day.

  • Ages 2 to 3TwosPointing, naming, matching colors, simple songs and short, hands-on turns.
  • Ages 3 to 4ThreesRetelling, counting, sorting, asking questions and practicing routines on their own.
  • Ages 4 to 5Pre-KLetters and sounds, counting and patterns, predicting and testing, and kindergarten-readiness skills.
