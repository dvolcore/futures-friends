Why families choose us

What we hold ourselves to

Futures Learning Center is where the Futures Friends program runs first. Everything we teach is meant to be tested here first, with real children and real families.

1

Curriculum-based care

Lessons for twos, threes and pre-K, planned as twelve monthly units with a readiness checklist before kindergarten. Eleven of the twelve months are written day by day as drafts, and none is approved yet.

2

Purposeful play

Four learning zones, outdoor time twice a day, music, cooking and art. It looks like play because it is; the teacher knows what each activity is for.

3

Teacher connection

Small groups in Missouri ratios, Futures Friends Academy training as its courses are ready, and a daily update in the Family App once the Futures Hub is live.

Daily life

What a day looks like

A sample day in the Threes Room; times are a sample, so ask us for the current schedule. Every room follows the same rhythm, adjusted for age.

  1. 7:00
    Arrival and free choiceA teacher greets your child at the door and you sign in; children choose a learning zone while friends arrive. Tablet sign-in comes with the app pilot.
  2. 8:00
    BreakfastFamily-style breakfast from the Eat the Rainbow menu, served at child-sized tables.
  3. 8:45
    Circle time and episodeSongs and the friend of the week. Short Futures Friends episodes join circle time once they are finished; none is yet.
  4. 9:15
    Learning zonesReading, Calm Corner, Discovery and Movement zones, with a teacher-led small group.
  5. 10:30
    Outdoor playClimbing, running, gardening and nature detective walks, weather permitting.
  6. 11:30
    LunchA hot lunch planned around the CACFP meal pattern. Children serve themselves with help and are invited to look at, smell or taste one new color. Saying no thanks is fine.
  7. 12:30
    Rest timeLights low, soft music and a blanket from home. Quiet books for early risers.
  8. 2:30
    SnackA rainbow snack and water, then a story with Booker.
  9. 3:00
    Afternoon zones and outdoor timeArt, building, music and a second outdoor block.
  10. 4:30
    PickupA teacher tells you about the day and gives you one question to ask at dinner. The Family App daily update comes with the app pilot.

Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions

What are your hours?

The center is open Monday to Friday. Call (816) 988-5661 for current drop-off and pickup times.

What ages do you serve?

Futures Friends is piloting here, so call (816) 988-5661 for the ages and rooms open right now. The program is written for ages 2 to 5 in a twos room, a threes room and a pre-K room. Rooms follow Missouri ratios: 1 adult for every 8 two-year-olds and 1 for every 10 three- and four-year-olds.

What does tuition include?

Tuition is $210 a week and includes breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack planned around the USDA CACFP meal pattern and the Futures Friends curriculum for your child's age (Unit 1 is written; later units are added as they are written). The family welcome kit and the Family App are planned and come to families when they are ready.

How much screen time will my child have?

Futures Friends schedules at most 24 minutes of episodes a week for children 3 to 5: one episode of up to 6 minutes, Monday to Thursday, watched together with a teacher, and never more than 30 minutes a week. Friday has no episode. All screens together in care stay at or under 30 minutes a week (the CDC early care and education standard), and children 2 and younger do not use screens in care. No episode is made yet; once they are, you will see every episode in the Family App.

How does the waitlist work?

Online applications are not switched on yet. Call (816) 988-5661 or email info@futureslearningcenter.com and the center will take your details and talk about next steps. A request is not a guaranteed spot. When a spot opens, the center calls families in order of application date.

Join the team that runs Futures Friends first. Every role includes paid Futures Friends Academy training and a clear path through the credential ladder.

Lead Teacher

Plan and lead the learning loop for the Threes or Pre-K Room.

Assistant Teacher

Partner with the lead teacher, run zones and support small groups.

Eat the Rainbow Cook

Prepare breakfast, lunch and snack designed around the CACFP meal pattern for the whole center, following each child's director-approved meal instruction.

Floater and Substitute
